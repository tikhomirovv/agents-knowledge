# B2B sales frameworks: WWW (3 whys) + A→C transformation

## Description
Core sales mental models for founders: the WWW framework (why anything/why now/why you), selling the A→C transformation (not features), and adapting the pitch to different buying-committee roles.

## Main sections
### WWW framework (three “whys”)
Before you sell, make sure the buyer can answer:

- **Why anything?**: Is there a real problem worth solving?
- **Why now?**: Why is it urgent *now* (trigger, deadline, risk, opportunity)?
- **Why you?**: Why this solution vs alternatives (do nothing, internal build, competitor)?

Use WWW as:
- discovery structure,
- demo framing,
- objection diagnosis (missing “why”).

### Selling transformation (A→C), not the product (B)
Buyers buy a change:

- **A**: current state (pain, waste, risk)
- **B**: your product (a bridge)
- **C**: desired state (outcome, KPI improvement, safety)

Sales job:
1. Make A vivid and expensive.
2. Make C concrete and attractive.
3. Use B only as proof that the bridge works.

### Emotional first, rational second
Decisions are often made emotionally (relief, fear of loss, status, safety) and then justified rationally.

Practical pattern:
- Start with A→C narrative (emotion).
- Then provide ROI math, specs, security, integrations (rationalization).

### Buying committee “languages”
Different roles care about different outcomes.

Translate your pitch by role:
- **CTO/CIO**: integration, security, reliability, time-to-implement (SLA, API, RTO/RPO).
- **CFO**: ROI, payback, TCO, risk cost.
- **CEO**: strategic advantage, growth, speed, leadership.
- **End users**: usability, time saved, daily workflow.

## Practical examples
### Example 1 — reframing features into A→C
Feature: “AI parses CVs.”

- A: “HR spends 3 hours/day formatting and misses good candidates.”
- C: “15 minutes/day processing, fewer errors, less burnout.”
- Proof: “AI parser standardizes in 6 seconds with audit trail.”

- If a deal stalls, check which “why” is weak; fix that before adding more features.

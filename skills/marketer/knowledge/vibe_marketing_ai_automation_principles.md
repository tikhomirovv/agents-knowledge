# Vibe Marketing: AI automation principles for small teams

## Description
How to use AI to compress marketing workflows (research → creative iterations → analysis) with a tiny team: focus on speed, experiments, and direct feedback loops — without relying on gray/ToS‑breaking tactics.

## Main sections
### What “Vibe Marketing” means (operationally)
Marketing where you:
- keep strategy and taste human-owned,
- automate repetitive work with AI,
- ship experiments fast,
- measure and iterate.

### Why traditional marketing org charts often slow you down
Failure mode:
- many layers between “idea” and “tested creative”
- long approvals → slow learning

Vibe alternative:
- compact team focused on **strategy + creative hypotheses + measurement**.

### The core loop: hypothesis → variant generation → small test → scale
Default weekly loop:
1. Pick 1–2 ICP pains (hypotheses).
2. Generate 10–30 creative variants (hooks, scripts, angles).
3. Test on small budgets.
4. Keep winners and scale; archive losers.
5. Update your “angle bank” and “hook bank”.

### Competitive creative research (ethical pattern mining)
Goal:
- understand what messages/angles already work in the market,
- generate differentiated variants.

Rules:
- Don’t copy ads 1:1.
- Extract patterns: hook type, emotional driver, proof type, CTA style.

### What to automate first
High ROI automation targets:
- competitor creative cataloging (manual or tool-assisted)
- tagging creatives by angle/pain/emotion
- generating copy variants (titles, thumbnails text, hook lines)
- summarizing test results into decisions (“scale/kill/iterate”)

### Retention curves as a creative diagnostic
For video, look at the retention curve:
- sharp early drop ⇒ hook mismatch
- mid-video collapse ⇒ unclear value/story
- strong end ⇒ CTA timing or offer clarity

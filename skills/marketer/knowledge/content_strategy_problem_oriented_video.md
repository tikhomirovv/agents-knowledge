# Problem-oriented content strategy (short video + channel)

## Description
How to choose between entertainment vs problem-oriented content, structure a conversion-friendly content mix, and research pains to generate repeatable topics that sell.

## Main sections
### Two master content verticals
1. **Entertainment / procrastination**
   - Dominates views, usually weak conversion for non-entertainment products.
2. **Problem-oriented content**
   - Lower views, higher conversion and higher audience loyalty when it targets a real pain.

Decision rule:
- If you need sales/leads, prioritize **problem-oriented** content.

### The “problem → consequence → solution” spine
Baseline structure:
1. State the problem in user language.
2. Intensify consequences (without fear-mongering).
3. Provide a clear solution path (framework, steps, or your product).

### A practical content mix (default)
Use this as a starting allocation (adjust by niche):
- **30%** educational (tied to pains)
- **30%** engaging/interactive (still tied to pains)
- **20%** reputation/proof (cases, numbers, audits, teardown)
- **20%** direct selling

### How to research pains (repeatable method)
1. Define a narrow ICP segment.
2. Collect pains from:
   - comments on competitor posts
   - forums/communities
   - search queries
   - customer interviews
3. Categorize pains:
   - emotional
   - functional
   - social/status
   - financial
4. Turn each pain into 5–10 “problem statements” suitable for hooks.

### What not to post (B2B default)
Low-signal corporate content (office life, internal news) usually does not warm buyers. Prefer:
- insights
- data
- mistakes + fixes
- case studies
- checklists

## Practical examples
### Example 1 — turning a pain into a series
Pain: “We waste ad budget and can’t explain CAC.”
Series:
- “Why your CAC lies to you”
- “3 levers that actually drop CAC”
- “Creative tests: what to keep/kill”
- “Payback period in 5 minutes”

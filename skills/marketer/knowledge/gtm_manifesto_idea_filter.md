# GTM manifesto: filtering ideas before you buy traffic

## Description
A lightweight “GTM manifesto” concept: document ICP + problem + value/ROI + pricing sanity before running acquisition tests, so you don’t waste cycles on ideas that can’t work economically.

## Main sections
### The failure mode: random offer testing
“Run ads until you get N signups and only then decide” works, but it’s noisy and wasteful if:
- ICP is vague,
- the problem is weak,
- pricing is disconnected from value.

### GTM manifesto as a “precision tool”
Goal: tighten your target area before testing.

Minimum GTM manifesto fields:
1. **ICP**: who buys, context, constraints.
2. **Problem**: what pain is acute and expensive.
3. **Solution narrative**: why your mechanism solves it.
4. **ROI structure**: how value is created (time saved, revenue gained, risk reduced).
5. **Channels**: where ICP lives and how you can reach them.

### Idea due diligence checklist (kill fast)
Reject/iterate if:
- ICP is “everyone”.
- the problem is “nice to have”.
- ROI can’t be explained in numbers.
- price is 10× too high/low for the segment.

### Testing becomes “sniper”, not “shotgun”
With a decent manifesto:
- creative is clearer,
- offers are sharper,
- CAC experiments converge faster.

## Practical examples

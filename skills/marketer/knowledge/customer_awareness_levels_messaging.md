# Customer awareness levels and messaging (Schwartz model)

## Description
How to adapt marketing messages, content, and CTAs to the customer’s awareness level (unaware → most aware). Useful for landing pages, ads, email sequences, webinars, and content strategy.

## Main sections
### Core idea
Customers buy a transition:
- **A** (current state) → **C** (desired state)
Your product is the bridge, but customers differ in how much they understand:
- the problem,
- solution categories,
- your product,
- and why to buy now.

### The 5 awareness levels
1. **Unaware**
   - doesn’t recognize a problem.
2. **Problem aware**
   - feels pain, doesn’t know solutions.
3. **Solution aware**
   - knows solution types exist, doesn’t know your product.
4. **Product aware**
   - knows your product, unsure why you vs alternatives.
5. **Most aware**
   - almost ready; needs final friction removal / terms / incentive.

### What to say at each level (practical defaults)
**Unaware**
- Goal: create problem awareness.
- Content: research, “hidden cost” stories, provocative data.
- CTA: subscribe, read guide, take quiz.

**Problem aware**
- Goal: frame the problem and consequences; introduce solution existence.
- Content: diagnostics, calculators, “10 signs”, case studies of pain.
- CTA: download checklist, join webinar, get benchmark.

**Solution aware**
- Goal: differentiate your solution category.
- Content: category comparisons, implementation guides, “how to choose”.
- CTA: request demo, start trial, get implementation plan.

**Product aware**
- Goal: differentiate your product (proof, unique mechanisms, ROI).
- Content: comparisons vs competitors, teardown, proof assets.
- CTA: demo + ROI estimate, security/IT doc, pricing talk.

**Most aware**
- Goal: remove last blockers.
- Content: onboarding path, SLA/terms, migration support, guarantees.
- CTA: “start now”, annual discount, procurement-ready docs.

### Metrics to focus on by level
Map metrics to intent:
- Unaware: reach, watch time, subscriptions.
- Problem aware: lead capture rate, webinar signups.
- Solution aware: demo/trial requests.
- Product aware: sales-qualified leads, win rate.
- Most aware: close rate, time-to-close.

## Practical examples
### Example 1 — same product, different message
Product: B2B automation SaaS.

- Unaware ad: “Teams lose 20 hours/month to manual work (data).”
- Problem aware: “Calculate your hidden cost in 30 seconds.”
- Solution aware: “Automation vs outsourcing: what wins.”
- Product aware: “Why our approach reduces errors by \(X\)%.”
- Most aware: “Implementation in 7 days, migration included.”

## Notes
- Don’t send “buy now” messages to unaware audiences; it reads like spam and inflates CAC.

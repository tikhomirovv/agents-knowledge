# Video scriptwriting for marketing (VSL, ads, education)

## Description
Script frameworks for marketing video: hook types, mapping scripts to GTM, and a practical 3‑act structure for everything from 15‑second ads to VSLs.

## Main sections
### Formats and when to use them
- **Ad video (15–60s)**: capture attention + drive a single click/action.
- **Short organic (TikTok/Reels/Shorts)**: reach + awareness + soft CTA.
- **Educational video**: trust + onboarding + objection handling.
- **VSL (2–20 min)**: direct conversion on a landing page.

### GTM → script mapping
Treat GTM as the “source of truth” for your scripts:
- **ICP** → protagonist (“hero”)
- **Pain** → conflict
- **Value proposition** → resolution
- **Economic value** → rational motivation
- **Emotional value** → emotional motivation

### Hook types (fast defaults)
Use one:
- shocking statistic (credible)
- provocative claim
- unexpected question
- paradox / contrarian take

### 3‑act structure (works across lengths)
**Hook (0–5%)**
- earn attention in 1–3 seconds
- signal relevance

**Act 1 — Setup (10–25%)**
- introduce ICP context
- show the problem in a scene

**Act 2 — Escalation (50–60%)**
- deepen consequences
- show failed attempts / obstacles
- “point of no return”

**Act 3 — Resolution (20–30%)**
- introduce solution
- show transformation “before/after”
- show results (numbers, proof)
- CTA: single next step

### Emotional drivers in B2B
Even in B2B, decisions are strongly emotional. Common drivers:
- fear of reputational loss
- desire for personal success/recognition
- safety/predictability
- avoidance of risky/complex choices

## Practical examples
### Example 1 — 30s ad skeleton
Hook: “Most teams lose 30% budget here”
Setup: show messy spreadsheet + stress
Escalation: “we tried dashboards, it got worse”
Resolution: show 1 clear workflow + result number
CTA: “Get the template / book demo”

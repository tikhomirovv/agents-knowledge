# Short video hooks playbook (3-second attention)

## Description
How to design hooks for short-form video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) that win the first 3 seconds, avoid clickbait decay, and improve both organic distribution and paid CTR.

## Main sections
### Why hooks matter
In short video, the decision to continue watching is made in **~3 seconds**. Hooks can multiply views and distribution, but only if the rest of the video delivers.

### Two approaches: borrowed hooks vs authored hooks
**Borrowed hooks**
- Pros: fast, proven attention.
- Cons: low trust, “hook drop-off” (people leave after the hook), reputational risk if mismatch.

**Authored hooks**
- Pros: better long-term conversion and brand signal.
- Cons: requires system and iteration.

Decision rule:
- If you care about conversion/brand, optimize for authored hooks and keep borrowed patterns only as references.

### Formula for an authored hook
**Strong visual + emotional sound + ultra-short text** → hook.

Constraints:
- Text: **3–5 words**
- Visual: high contrast, movement, face/emotion when relevant
- Sound: recognizable trigger (notification, snap, “whoosh”), or tension-building

### Hook text templates (safe defaults)
Use these as starting points; adapt to the product pain:
- “Only \(X%\) know this”
- “Hidden reason you \[fail\]”
- “Never do this…”
- “You’re losing money here”
- “Stop \[common mistake\]”
- “\(N\) ways to \[result\]”

### Don’t confuse high CTR with high conversion
Some hook types inflate CTR but kill downstream conversion (e.g., pure sexual bait for unrelated products).

Optimization order:
1. Hook that matches your ICP pain
2. Retention through the core message
3. Clear CTA aligned with the hook promise

### A hook creation process (repeatable)
1. **Define the offer emotion** (fear of loss, relief, pride, safety, belonging).
2. **Pick the pain** (1 sentence, user language).
3. **Sketch 3 visual concepts** (simple, high contrast).
4. **Select sound** that amplifies emotion.
5. **Write 10 micro-text variants** (3–5 words).
6. **Test** (organic or small paid budget).
7. **Keep winners**, kill losers fast.

## Practical examples
### Example 1 — B2B pain hook
Text: “90% marketers lose money”
Visual: graph down → graph up
Sound: drop + “whoosh”
Why it works: professional loss aversion + contrast problem→solution.

### Example 2 — credibility-preserving hook
Instead of: “Get rich in 24 hours”
Use: “Stop wasting ad budget”
(Same emotional trigger; less likely to feel scammy and more platform-safe.)

## Notes
- A hook is a contract. If the video breaks the contract, you get drop-off, negative comments, and worse future distribution.

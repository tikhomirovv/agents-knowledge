# B2C ideation and validation (landing + traffic tests)

## Description
Why classic interviews (“custdev”) often fail in B2C, and how to validate B2C ideas via landing-page + traffic tests before building. Includes what metrics to measure and why.

## Main sections
### The B2C problem: people say one thing, do another
In B2C, self-reports are often unreliable. The safer path is to validate with behavior:
- do they click?
- do they leave email?
- do they attempt to buy?

### Validate with traffic before product
Default B2C validation artifact:
- landing page describing the promise
- price anchor (plans/pricing)
- CTA (email waitlist, “download”, fake checkout)
- a short demo video can work instead of product

### What can kill B2C ideas early
- auction competition: you may compete with giants for the same audience
- small price points require 1-touch conversion
- unit economics won’t work even if the problem is real

### Metrics to watch (directional)
Track at minimum:
- **CTR** of the ad creative
- **Landing conversion** (email/signup/purchase intent)
- **Estimated CAC** (spend / conversions)
- **Estimated LTV** (pricing assumptions)

Decision rule:
- if CAC can’t ever be paid back by plausible LTV, stop or reposition.

### A simple B2C validation loop
1. pick problem + solution angle
2. build landing (with pricing)
3. run small traffic tests
4. iterate message/price/segment
5. only then build MVP
